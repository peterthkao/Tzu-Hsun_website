Links

Useful Links

A curated collection of satellite missions, data archives, and space weather services relevant to my research in ionospheric and magnetospheric science.

Satellite Missions
COSMO — CubeSat for Observations of Signatures in the Magnetosphere and Orbit
A 6U CubeSat developed at CU Boulder measuring Earth's magnetic field using vectorized rubidium scalar magnetometers.
Swarm — Earth Online (ESA)
ESA's constellation of three satellites measuring Earth's magnetic field and its interactions with the ionosphere.
Van Allen Probes (NASA / JHU APL)
NASA twin-spacecraft mission that explored Earth's radiation belts and the inner magnetosphere dynamics.
ERG Science Center (Nagoya University / JAXA)
Science center for Japan's Arase (ERG) satellite, studying energization and radiation in Earth's inner magnetosphere.
Data Centers & Archives
SPDF — Coordinated Data Analysis Web (CDAWeb)
NASA's primary portal for accessing, visualizing, and analyzing heliospheric and magnetospheric space physics data.
TACC — Taiwan Data Process Center (CWA)
Taiwan Atmospheric and Climate Center providing atmospheric observation, ionospheric, and remote sensing data products.
Taiwan Seismological and Geophysical Data Management System (CWA)
Taiwan earthquake and geophysical data management system, providing seismological and ionospheric observation records.
NASA CCMC — Community Coordinated Modeling Center
NASA center supporting the development, testing, and dissemination of space weather models and simulation tools.
Space Weather Services
Real-time Dst Index (WDC, Kyoto University)
World Data Center for Geomagnetism real-time Disturbance Storm Time index, tracking geomagnetic storm intensity.
Kp Index (GFZ German Research Centre for Geosciences)
Official source for the global geomagnetic activity Kp index, produced by GFZ Potsdam from ground-based magnetometer networks.
Space Weather Enthusiasts Dashboard (NOAA / NWS SWPC)
NOAA's interactive dashboard displaying real-time solar wind, geomagnetic indices, and aurora activity for space weather monitoring.
Agency & University
NASA — National Aeronautics and Space Administration
U.S. federal agency leading civilian space exploration, aeronautics research, and Earth and planetary science programs.
Space Weather Prediction Center (SWPC, NOAA / NWS)
NOAA's official center for space weather forecasts, alerts, and warnings, serving government, public safety, and commercial users.
Taiwan Space Agency (TASA)
Taiwan's national space agency overseeing satellite development, space science missions, and international aerospace cooperation.
Space Weather Operational Office (SWOO, CWA)
Taiwan's national space weather monitoring, forecasting, and warning service operated by the Central Weather Administration.
Dept. of Space Science & Engineering — National Central University
Taiwan's premier department for space science and engineering education, with strengths in ionospheric physics, remote sensing, and satellite systems.
Ann and H.J. Smead Aerospace Engineering Sciences — University of Colorado Boulder
CU Boulder's aerospace department, home to cutting-edge research in astrodynamics, space weather, CubeSats, and planetary science.
